What it does

I run more than one bot, and I got tired of repeating context and losing track of what still needed my OK. I wrote this skill so one primary bot routes every request to the right specialist, writes a handoff note, and doesn’t do the specialist’s work itself.

Each handoff says what the task is, what’s done, what’s left, which files matter, the deadline, and what still needs your approval. Once a week the coordinator does a read-only review and flags routines that produce nothing and handoffs that bounced.

Who it’s for

People with two or more bots, each with its own job, who want one place to send requests. If you’re still on one bot, the free Bot Safety Rules Starter on GitHub has the standing safety rules and a blank handoff note to start with.

FAQ

Do I need more than one bot?

Yes. The coordinator can only hand work to specialists that actually exist and that you’ve described to it.

Will the coordinator send messages or spend money?

The standing rules tell it not to send email or messages without your approval and not to spend money. You can toggle those two rules. They’re instructions, so keep your platform’s own approval controls on too.

Does the weekly review change anything?

No. It’s read-only. Pausing or deleting a routine is always your call.

What happens when lanes overlap?

Overlapping lanes produce bounced or duplicate handoffs, so give each specialist one clear job.

What do I get when I buy it?

A .zip with SKILL.md and a setup README.
