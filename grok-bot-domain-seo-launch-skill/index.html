What it does

I wrote this skill after moving a static site from its free host address to a custom domain with a bot doing most of the work. It turns that run into one ordered playbook. The bot finishes and verifies each phase before it starts the next, and it keeps a running learnings file as it goes.

It stops for you at every payment, login, and publishing step. It scans outgoing files for personal details. It never invents ratings, reviews, or numbers, and it never hides text aimed at crawlers.

Who it’s for

People launching a static site or a small brand on a custom domain, with an AI bot helping. You don’t need to be an SEO expert. You buy the domain, handle the logins, and approve each step.

The 8 phases

  1. Pick the host and set up DNS: registrar cleanup, host records, domain verification, HTTPS, and email DNS
  2. Cut over the URLs: canonicals, OG tags, JSON-LD, sitemap, robots.txt, and internal links, then verify the 301s
  3. Search engines: a domain property, sitemap submission, Change of Address, webmaster tools import, and IndexNow
  4. On-page foundations: titles, headings, utility pages, llms.txt, favicon, schema, internal links, speed, and query overlap
  5. Cookieless analytics: a separate brand account and a script snippet, with no DNS change
  6. Research and write the launch batch: about 15 to 20 pages, each with one target query and an angle drawn from what ranks now
  7. Publish: upload order, byte-for-byte checks, build status, and pings
  8. Distribution and measurement: other channels, a steady cadence, and a 90-day review

FAQ

Will the bot buy the domain or pay for anything?

No. Buying the domain, paying for anything, and registrar logins are owner-only steps. The skill tells the bot to stop at every payment, payout, bank, or tax step.

Does it guarantee rankings or traffic?

No. It sets up the foundations and a first batch of pages. Rankings depend on your site, your niche, and the search engines.

Which hosts does it cover?

It’s written for static sites on GitHub Pages or a similar static host. A CMS or app host needs some steps adapted, and the skill has a short note on moving DNS to a CDN later.

What do I still do myself?

Buy the domain, pay for anything, sign in to the registrar, host, search console, and analytics accounts, handle 2FA, and approve each phase and every page before it goes live.

What do I get when I buy it?

A .zip with one SKILL.md and a README.
